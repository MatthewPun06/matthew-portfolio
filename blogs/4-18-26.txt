Warm light scatters across my friends as we sit around a circle of stones under the moon. As the spirit of red and orange emerges from the tent of branches, twigs, and leaves, it casts a blanket of warmth and comfort over us in the depths of these dark woods. Its gentle crackling creates a bed of feathers for our voices to lay on as we share stories in the warm light. Yet the most peculiar thing about the flickering flames is the amount of attention and care it needs, despite how dangerous it can be if it grows too large.
The process is simple: Allow the kindling to catch. Then the tinder. And finally the logs. Give it a home and nurture it, but always remember to give it space. You can also speed up each step–building a tent out of logs, stacking sticks against the logs, and crushing up the crunchiest leaves into the middle–but the process remains the same.
As I sit by the fire, its flames burn high and mighty as I carefully construct its home and lay in tinder to keep it fueled. Its walls allow it to breathe and dance, lighting up the forest around it and creating an aegis of comfort. Hypnotized by its dancing and flickering, I take a step back to enjoy the flames and the warmth they provide. Yet, after a while, the logs that once housed the heart eventually smothers it after collapsing.
As the forest dims to be lit by the stars and moon, the cold returns to fill the void. The fire’s movement and vitality has faded away, but its warmth and its will to live remain. Building it a new home and providing kindling again, the embers start to birth a new flame under a new roof. As I continue to fuel this fire, it’s easier to bring it back and nurture it to grow larger under my wing. But this time, its mesmerizing dancing keeps me near, allowing me to tend to it while still having the chance to enjoy the warmth, light, and safety it provides under its aegis.
