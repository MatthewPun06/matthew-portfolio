I still write with purple prose,
I still get a blood pressure spike when someone invites me to do something new,
I still listen to the same music I did 6 years ago,
I still clench my jaw to stop myself from yawning during boring lectures and conversations,
and I still pretend to be on my phone to not seem lonely or out of place.
But,
I don’t still always say no to invitations to try something new,
I don’t still play video games for 10 hours a day,
I don’t still reject new foods,
I don’t still complain about waking up early,
and I don’t still hide who I am to my closest friends.
And,
I still smile uncontrollably when I’m with my friends,
I still love playing, listening, and composing music,
I still explore the curiosities of the world around me,
I still lose track of time with running,
and I still set aside time to just be alone.
So,
I will.
Still, am I still?
