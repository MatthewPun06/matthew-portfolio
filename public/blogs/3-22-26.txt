A while back, I saw a reel about viewing relationships from the lens of trains.
There are some people who get on the same train, talk for a minute, and leave to live the rest of their life. Others may stay on for chapters of your life, but they will still need to depart on their own path. And then you have people who stay on that train for life with you. They are meant to be your closest, choosing to take the same connections until you step off. And yet, there are still millions of people on that train and other trains that you pass by.
This week, someone came into my life for a short stop, and, during the conversation, they told me they disliked the beach, hiking, and the outdoors. I found myself wanting to show them what I loved about nature, but some people are meant to catch a connection that will get them closer to their own destination, even if I want them to stay just a little longer. Their connection came, and as they stepped off, I was left with the time we spent and the book they left on their seat.
In my childhood, I had a friend take a connection before returning back to my car in high school. Even after 5 years, we still remembered how to dance together, but we’ve come to understand that we will be the people who need to take connecting trains at every turn. There will still be times that we share a stop or two to catch up before going back to our own paths. Yet, after every one, they always manage to leave a souvenir from their travels next to me.
However, for whoever stays on the train with me until the very end, I’ll decorate the train a little bit. After all, I’ll be here the whole time. So until we reach our destination, it might be nice to fog the windows for some drawings, add pillows to the seats, and decorate the walls. Spending time in a cozy home is easier than in the cold, so I might as well turn it into something to love and enjoy before stepping off to the next destination.
